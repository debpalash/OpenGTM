# YUPCHA MASTER ROADMAP — THE OPEN-SOURCE CLAY KILLER

## NORTH STAR POSITIONING

**"The self-hosted GTM data engine that shows you the bill before you run it. Your keys, your VPC, zero markup, driven from your terminal, your agent, or your spreadsheet."**

Yupcha does not compete with Clay on data supply (Clay's real moat is provider licensing — the correct HN objection at news.ycombinator.com/item?id=43756298). Yupcha sells the orchestration layer Clay taxes at 15–30x: waterfalls, the learned cost planner, spend estimates, budget ceilings, and audited agentic access — at zero margin over data users buy directly. Every headline claim is structural, i.e. Clay cannot copy it without destroying revenue: (1) BYOK at direct cost (Clay now meters Actions even on BYOK — the #1 Pricing-3.0 grievance), (2) never pay for unvalidated data or the same fact twice, (3) in-your-VPC data sovereignty with per-fact license provenance, (4) headless REST/MCP/n8n operation (Clay has no public API; their ex-largest user churned to a $200/mo Claude Code sub), (5) AI columns on your own GPU at $0 marginal cost. This is n8n-vs-Zapier's structural pricing wedge replayed against a $3.1B incumbent whose #1 churn driver is billing.

## CONFLICT RESOLUTIONS (explicit)

- **Grid feel: launch blocker (A) vs. post-launch (B) vs. floor-only (C).** Resolved: split. Launch is won by the wedge (cost/BYOK/API), not grid parity — the launch cohort is HN self-hosters, more forgiving than Clay power users. But retention dies in the first hour of real work. Server-side pagination + streaming rows ship at 3–6mo because the agency design-partner persona (B's multiplier) runs 10k-row campaigns. Deep 50k UI scale → 6–12mo, demand-gated.
- **Research/Claygent: quarter-one (C) vs. months 3–6 (A).** Resolved: LLM registry unification + Ollama/vLLM/OpenAI/Azure/Bedrock lands in the launch quarter because "Claygent on your own GPU" is a launch headline (C). ## WHAT ALL THREE STRATEGISTS MISSED (completeness check — added to plan)

1. **Provider-ToS vs. the cache pitch.** "Never pay for the same fact twice / enriched once, free forever" collides with vendor licensing: PDL, Apollo, Hunter et al. impose data-retention/caching limits in their ToS. Unbounded identity-cache retention of BYOK vendor data could get *users'* vendor accounts terminated and Yupcha branded unsafe. Fix: per-provider `cache_ttl`/retention declarations in the manifest schema (P1.6), enforced by the identity cache, surfaced in provenance. Turns a liability into a compliance feature.
2. **GDPR/CCPA controller posture.** Self-hosters become data controllers of scraped EU personal data. Ship: legitimate-interest guidance doc (B2B lead data), configurable data-retention TTLs, the erasure endpoint (P2.3) marketed as DSAR support, and — before cloud GA — a DPA template + subprocessor list. Also PECR/ePrivacy note for cold email in the outreach docs (CAN-SPAM is already enforced in code; EU rules are stricter).
3. **Reacher is itself AGPL/commercial dual-licensed.** Bundling it in compose is fine for self-host; *hosting it in Yupcha Cloud* likely needs a commercial license from Reacher. Audit all bundled service licenses (JobSpy, staffspy, Patchright) before cloud beta. Also: Reacher's port-25 SMTP probes from users' IPs risk blacklisting — add a deliverability warning in docs (support-ticket generator otherwise).
4. **The platform mapper returned a placeholder ("Test").** No independent audit of workspace isolation/auth/billing actually happened, and the README itself says "treat as single-tenant." Do not trust WI-1/2/3 as "landed" — commission an external security review of RLS + MCP write gates as a hard gate before cloud beta (also feeds the durability metric).
5. **Telemetry privacy.** Every success metric below depends on opt-in instance pings. Design it PostHog-style: anonymous, documented, one env var, source-visible payload — or the HN crowd that the launch targets will torch the trust story the metrics were meant to prove.

## TOP 5 RISKS

1. **The HN objection is simply correct at scale** — orchestration commoditizes (FullEnrich/BetterContact/pipe0), data licensing stays the moat, and the wedge never converts past hobbyists. *Mitigation:* meta-waterfall manifests ride the commoditizers; the kill-signal metric (manifest PRs <5 by mo-6) forces the cloud-first pivot before the runway burns.
2. **Execution bandwidth / bus factor ≈ 1.** The artifact trail (hardcoded /Users paths, placeholder platform audit, dual code paths) says single-dev; this roadmap is ~3–5 eng-years. *Mitigation:* the kill list is half the plan; community conversion is front-loaded (manifests/templates are contribution-shaped); agencies as design partners double as contributors.
