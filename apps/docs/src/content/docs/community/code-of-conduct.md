OpenGTM adopts the
[Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html).
The full text lives in
[`CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`](https://github.com/debpalash/opengtm/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

In short: be respectful, be constructive, assume good faith, and keep the
project a welcoming place for people of every background and experience level.

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may be
reported to the maintainers at **conduct@yupcha.com**. All complaints are
reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and reporters' privacy is
respected.
